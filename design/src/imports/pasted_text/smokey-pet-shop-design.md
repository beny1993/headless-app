Design a complete premium Persian RTL ecommerce website and admin dashboard for a pet shop brand called Smokey.

1. Brand Context

Brand name: Smokey

Industry: Premium pet products and pet lifestyle

Primary language: Persian

Layout direction: RTL — Right to Left

Primary audience:

* Cat and dog owners
* Mostly mobile-first users
* People who care deeply about their pets and consider them family
* Customers who value trusted recommendations, quality products, and beautiful design

The design must NOT look like a generic ecommerce marketplace.

Avoid:

* Dense product grids
* Marketplace-style layouts
* Excessive discount badges
* Generic blue ecommerce UI
* Overloaded navigation
* Too many bordered cards
* Cheap or childish pet-shop aesthetics

The experience should feel like a combination of:

* Premium lifestyle magazine
* Modern boutique ecommerce
* Warm pet community
* Expert pet-care guide
* Highly polished contemporary digital product

The core emotional feeling should be:

Warm, trustworthy, playful, premium, calm, and deeply connected to pets.

⸻

2. Creative Concept

Create the website around the concept:

“Life with Smokey”

The website should feel like entering Smokey’s world rather than entering a normal online shop.

Products should be presented in the context of real pet life:

* Eating
* Playing
* Sleeping
* Grooming
* Traveling
* Health and wellness
* Living together at home

Instead of organizing the entire experience only around product categories, combine:

Stories + Pet needs + Curated products + Education

Use editorial compositions, asymmetric layouts, large photography, oversized typography, horizontal storytelling sections, subtle illustrations, and playful micro-interactions.

The design should have personality but remain commercially effective.

15. RTL Requirements

RTL must be designed intentionally, not created by simply mirroring an LTR website.

Ensure:

* Correct Persian text alignment
* Logical navigation direction
* RTL carousels
* Correct breadcrumb direction
* Correct icons for next and previous actions
* Numbers and prices remain readable
* Form controls work correctly in mixed Persian and numeric content
* Tables remain usable
* Mobile gestures feel natural
* Persian typography has appropriate line height

Use Persian currency formatting consistently.
