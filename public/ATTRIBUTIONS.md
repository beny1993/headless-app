# Attributions — bundled imagery

Every image in `public/` is committed to this repo and served from our own
origin. Nothing here is hotlinked: external hosts are slow or unreachable from
Iranian IPs, and the platform is local-first. The one bundled font, Vazirmatn,
keeps its copyright notice and SIL OFL 1.1 licence in `src/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`,
beside the file that `src/lib/fonts.ts` loads.

## Images

The demo images below are flat illustrations generated for this project from
code (simple vector shapes rendered to JPEG). They carry no third-party
licence. They are stand-ins: a shop replaces them with its own photography
through the admin panel.
